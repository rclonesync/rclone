Application Privacy Policy

Application: Rclone
Effective date: 20-10-2026
Contact: rclonegdrivesync@gmail.com
Website: https://rclonesync.github.io/rclone/

1. Purpose and scope

The personal OAuth application named “Rclone” is maintained by the individual operator reachable at rclonegdrivesync@gmail.com. This application is separate from the rclone open-source project and is maintained for personal use with rclone and Google Drive OAuth. It authorizes rclone on a device controlled by the user to perform requested file operations. This policy covers that application and this informational website. rclone runs only on the user’s own computer and transfers data only between that computer and Google Drive. There is no operator-managed backend or other cloud storage destination.

2. Authorization and Google Drive permissions

Google handles sign-in and consent. The application does not ask for or store your Google password. OAuth tokens allow rclone to act within the permissions you grant. Access can include file contents and metadata such as names, IDs, sizes, timestamps, and folder structure, depending on the selected scope and operations.

The application requests the following Google permissions for personal file backup and synchronization. The Google consent screen shows the permissions requested at authorization:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive

Provides broad access to view, create, change, and delete Drive files. This supports personal backup, upload, download, and synchronization, including changes or deletions requested by the user. Access is not restricted to files created by this application.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.metadata.readonly

Allows reading information about Drive files, such as names, identifiers, sizes, and timestamps, for listing and comparing files. On its own this scope does not grant file-content access or write access. When combined with the broad drive scope above, it does not restrict the broader permission.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/docs

The Google consent configuration describes this permission as allowing the application to see, edit, create, and delete all of the user’s Google Drive files. It is used for the same user-directed backup and synchronization operations described above. This description reflects the operator’s reported consent configuration.

The application uses granted access for the file operations described here. A configured root folder does not narrow the OAuth permission itself. Broad file access supports synchronizing existing files and folders that were not created by this application. Read-only permissions cannot upload, change, or delete files, and metadata-only access cannot transfer contents.

3. How data is used and transferred

Drive data is used only to perform operations you initiate, such as listing, copying, downloading, uploading, or syncing files. Depending on the command and scope, operations may rename or delete data. rclone communicates with Google’s OAuth and Drive services over HTTPS. Transfers are limited to the user’s own computer and Google Drive; this application does not use another storage destination.

The application owner does not collect Drive files or tokens through this website, sell personal data, or share Google user data with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. Data is not used for advertising, profiling, or AI model training. Necessary communication with Google is limited to carrying out your authorization and file-transfer instructions. The operator does not provide a backend, sharing service, or telemetry service for this application.

4. Local tokens, storage, and security

In this application’s local setup, access and refresh tokens are stored in rclone’s configuration on the device where rclone is configured. Refresh tokens can allow continued access without signing in each time. The default configuration file is not encrypted at rest unless you enable rclone configuration encryption. Protect it using appropriate file permissions, device security, and encryption.

Configuration paths can vary with command options and environment variables on the user’s computer. Downloaded files, caches, logs, and backups may retain data on those systems. Credentials, tokens, configuration files, and sensitive logs should not be published or sent with support requests. No system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Retention, deletion, and revoking access

Local credentials remain until removed or replaced; their validity also depends on Google’s expiration and revocation rules. Local files, caches, logs, and backups remain according to your device and retention settings. Google Drive files remain until you remove them under Google’s retention rules.

You can revoke authorization in Google Account third-party connections. Remove the rclone remote or token from your configuration and separately delete any unwanted local copies, caches, logs, and backups. Removing local configuration alone does not revoke Google access, and revoking access does not delete transferred files. Contact rclonegdrivesync@gmail.com with privacy questions; never send passwords or tokens.

6. Website hosting and support messages

This static website contains no application analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, cookies, forms, or browser storage. It does not process OAuth callbacks. GitHub Pages, as the hosting provider, may process visitor information such as IP addresses and request logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Google processes data under its Privacy Policy. If you email support, the owner and email provider receive the message and any information you include; the owner uses it to respond and retains it only as needed for that purpose.

7. Policy changes and contact

The owner will update this page and its effective date when the app’s practices change. For questions about Rclone, contact rclonegdrivesync@gmail.com.

8. Data categories and processing purposes

  • File content: rclone reads or transfers the files selected by your commands for personal backup or synchronization. Google-native documents may be exported to supported formats when requested.
  • File and folder metadata: names, identifiers, paths, file sizes, modification times, and available checksums support listing, locating files, comparing versions, and deciding what to transfer. Relevant Drive or shared-drive identifiers may be used to select the configured storage location.
  • OAuth credentials: access tokens authorize API requests; refresh tokens obtain replacement access tokens. These credentials are used for authorization, not advertising or identifying users across websites.
  • Operational information: when local logging or caching is enabled, file paths, transfer results, errors, or file content may be written locally. The contents and retention depend on the rclone flags and device settings.

This personal Drive application does not request access to Gmail, Google Contacts, or Google Calendar for its file-transfer purpose. Any additional Google permissions must be disclosed before they are introduced.

9. Limited use of Google user data

Rclone’s use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the file-management functions described in this policy. It is not sold, used for targeted or personalized advertising, used to determine creditworthiness, or transferred to data brokers or information resellers.

Google Workspace API data is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. The operator does not routinely review file contents. You may access your own files on your own device; the operator receives support information only when you choose to send it. Please redact personal file content and credentials before requesting support.

10. Recipients and international processing

Google receives the requests and content necessary to authenticate and operate on your Drive files. In this local setup, the operator does not run a server that receives copies of your Drive data or tokens, and there are no advertising or analytics recipients. This application does not transfer files to another cloud storage provider. Google, GitHub, and the email provider may process information in countries other than your own under their applicable terms and privacy policies. No particular storage country is promised by this application.

11. Requests, deletion assistance, and changes

Send access, correction, or deletion questions to rclonegdrivesync@gmail.com with the subject “Rclone privacy request.” Identify the data or support correspondence concerned without providing passwords or tokens. The operator can address support messages in their possession but cannot directly erase files or credentials held only on your device or in your Google Account. You control those copies and can use the removal steps above.

Support correspondence is retained while necessary to resolve the request and then deleted when no longer needed, subject to any applicable retention obligations. The operator does not maintain a separate central archive of Google Drive files in this local setup. Backup copies on systems you control may persist until you remove or expire them. Revoking permission stops future authorized access but does not automatically remove previously transferred copies.

Material changes to the use or sharing of Google user data will be disclosed on this page before they take effect, and additional consent will be requested where required. The effective date identifies the applicable version. See also the Terms of Service.
